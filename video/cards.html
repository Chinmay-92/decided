Decided

The Unique Kernel

Most “AI meeting summary” tools stop at summarising. Decided keeps a decision log and, every time you capture a new thread, checks it against past decisions: “⚠ You may have decided this already.”

Who It’s For

A developer or tech lead on a small product team who makes architecture calls in chat threads.
